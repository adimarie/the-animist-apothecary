There is a particular threshold — when the gifts begin to wake, when the calling grows too loud to set down, when the work asks to come through you and into the world. That threshold is rarely meant to be crossed alone.

The Work

Ceremonially supported coaching.

Ceremonial coaching is concise, specialized development and guidance for those coming into their spiritual gifts — a private, one-to-one arc held over a season of change, not a single session — the rare seat that almost no one staffs.

The arc weaves two currents into one continuous container: ongoing guidance and counsel with Adi Marie, and — at the arc’s heart — the Luminous Breath, a Sacred Breath Session of deep spiritual union and purification, held in person and prepared for with reverent care. Neither current is an add-on to the other. The counsel prepares the ground and harvests what opens; the ceremony opens what counsel alone cannot reach.

Every session is ceremonially assisted — opened, held, and closed as ceremony, so the deep material is met in the field where it actually lives. This is not a quick fix, and it is not advice-giving. It is a living, breathing developmental arc — a container built together for the purpose of your becoming.

Adi Marie walks beside you as both guide and witness — tending the inner work, the ceremonial work, and the slow, deliberate work of bringing a gift into form here in this world.

The Shape of the Journey

Between the realms, into this one.

An arc moves through three living movements. They are not steps to complete, but currents to travel — the gift comes through the unseen, finds its form, and learns to live in your days.

First Movement

Between the Realms

The journeying work — tending what comes through from the unseen. Adi Marie accompanies you across the threshold so you are not crossing it alone, holding the ceremonial container as the gifts begin to wake and speak.

Second Movement

To Actualize

The gift takes form. We give shape, language, and discipline to what is emerging — clarifying the calling, building the practice, and refining the way the work wants to move through you.

Third Movement

To Embody

The calling comes home to your life. The work of bringing it down to earth — into your days, your relationships, your offering to the world — so the gift is not only received, but lived, here, in this world.

How the Work is Held

Two currents, held as one.

The rare thing about this seat is that both currents are tended by one practitioner. The counsel and the ceremony are not separate engagements — they are woven into a single, continuous arc, each making the other more powerful.

Guidance & Counsel

The grounded current

Ninety-minute counsel sessions serve as the integrative and preparatory anchor of the arc — helping the day-to-day you weave in what emerges when converged with the expansive innerworld. Rhythm, structure, and discernment; preparation before ceremony, witness and integration after. This is where what opens becomes something you live.

The Luminous Breath

The ceremonial current

The in-person heart of the arc — interdimensional ceremonial work of extraordinary depth. A held passage of union and purification that reaches beneath thought, beneath performance, beneath the learned self — and returns you to what you actually are.

The Ceremonial Heart

The Luminous Breath

A Sacred Breath Session of deep spiritual union and purification.

At the heart of this arc is a ceremony unlike anything else Adi Marie holds — and unlike anything most people will encounter in a lifetime. It is exquisitely simple and utterly vast. It is not a journey of visions and stories; it is direct contact with the core — the place beneath thought, beneath performance, beneath the learned self.

Each ceremony is a profound direct experience of Divine Union — a deep experiential initiation and calibration with the greater-than-human forces, powers, and beings that exist within and as the great oceans of existence, consecrating you into the sacred fields of your life. In a single held passage, what is not yours washes away, and what you are remains. Those who cross it return with a tenderness and clarity that reorders everything: your soul’s path and the living of your life, finally facing the same direction.

The skill of this passage is not doing it right. It is allowing. Softening. Letting go of steering — and letting truth arrive.

In the words of those who have crossed

“That’s what it would feel like if I were to become a shooting star… and then my soul was reborn.”

“I was held, dancing, suspended, within the dilated pupil of God.”

Prepared

The weeks before are a devotion of their own — orientation, quieting, turning inward. The body steadied, the heart made clear, the life simplified, so the threshold can be met with reverence.

Held

The session is held in person at The Haven — the Apothecary’s private sanctuary in the Topanga canyons — with consent, boundaries, and skilled somatic care woven before anything begins. You are accompanied the entire way.

Tended

Afterward comes the soft landing — aftercare, counsel, and the weeks of integration, where what is born is protected, witnessed, and slowly made livable.

This is not a one-night event. It is a living threshold — preparation shapes what becomes possible, and aftercare protects what is born.

The Shape of a Season

A season, given form.

Meetings every few weeks — a steady cadence, with room for the changes to move through your life. Counsel anchors the arc; ceremony stands at its heart; the whole season is shaped to you in the Conversation.

This arc is not designed for a singular breakthrough, but for steady transformation. Each session builds upon the last — not in pursuit of peak experiences, but to grow an ecosystem of sovereignty and inner tending: new foundational ground for you to live from.

Nothing Here Is in a Rush

The long arc.

The ceremony is not the end of the work — it is the opening of a gateway, the planting of a garden one returns to. Sacred seeds take years, sometimes decades, to bloom. This arc is built for that truth: a season of skilled counsel around the deepest ceremony the practice holds, so what opens has somewhere to live.

What tends to emerge, over the long arc, announces itself quietly — fewer compulsions, more honesty, a cleaner no, a different relationship with time. Nothing here is promised on a timeline. That is not a hedge; it is the difference.

“May your body be well tended. May your heart be clear enough to recognize what is true when it arrives.”

How the Season Unfolds

The seasons of the arc.

Developmental work moves in seasons. Every arc is unique to the one walking it, but there is a shape to how this depth of work tends to unfold — four living phases, traveled rather than checked off.

First Season

Arrival

Orienting to the container

The time of listening — to what brought you here, to what your body is holding, to what wants to shift. Trust is built with each other and with the process itself; rhythm, language, and shared ground are established. Excitement and vulnerability tend to arrive together. Both are welcome.

Second Season

Descent

Meeting what lives beneath

As trust deepens, the work moves toward what has been avoided, unfelt, or unnamed — the patterns that run you, the loyalties that bind you, the parts of yourself you have exiled. This is where the real medicine lives. Nothing is rushed here, and nothing is bypassed.

Third Season

Transformation

Working the material

Something begins to shift — not because the mind figured it out, but because your whole system is reorganizing around a new truth. Insight becomes embodied. New choices emerge, not from willpower, but from a genuinely different relationship with yourself and with life.

Fourth Season

Emergence

Stepping into what you are becoming

You begin to live from the new ground. Sessions grow lighter, more spacious, more celebratory. What has shifted is named and honored, and the transition out of the container is tended with care — the one you were hoping to become, no longer hoped for. Lived.

These phases are not linear. You may spiral through them more than once — descent and emergence at the same time. Trust the intelligence of the process.

What the Container Asks

The shape of the arc.

Every arc is shaped to the one person it holds. These are the principles the container is built on — the same whoever walks it.

  • Bespoke, never templated. No two arcs are alike. The work is shaped to your gifts, your threshold, and the calling that is actually coming through — not a curriculum you are fitted into.
  • A season, not a session. Real unfolding takes time. This is a sustained arc held over a season of change, with a rhythm of meetings rather than one-off appointments.
  • Concise and specialized. Focused, skilled guidance — nothing padded. Each meeting is precise to where you are and what the work asks next.
  • Both realms, one steward. The ceremonial and the practical are tended together, by one practitioner who can hold both without dropping either.
  • Small numbers, by design. Only a few arcs are held at a time, and the calendar for this work is often reserved months in advance. This is the lowest-volume, highest-depth work in the practice, and it is kept that way on purpose.
  • Reciprocity, honestly held. The container is entered through sacred reciprocity, with a sliding range so that depth of work is the deciding factor, not only means.

Held with a clear understanding: this is not medical or psychological treatment. It is a ceremonial and somatic journey into the depths of your being — slow, relational, and sacred.

Entering the Arc

A season shaped to you.

Length, rhythm, and reciprocity are settled together in the Conversation — because the arc is built around your threshold, not fitted to a package. Sacred Reciprocity holds the range, the season’s shape is agreed before any work begins, and only four seats are held each season. And if this is not yet your season — the open doorways are where the path usually begins.
