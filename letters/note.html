A Note

An open letter

Hello, friend —

I am opening up the seasonal letter of The Animist Apothecary to the people I have shared this work with. It is the formal home, now, of what has long been forming — a named threshold for a practice that has been held in close circles for many years.

The letter is where all of it lives: community gatherings and firesides, virtual and in person; the rituals and ceremonies we hold together; learning labs and workshops; and the travel ahead. Less an events calendar than a way to keep this work thrumming and alive in your own life — in your own home, in your own community, woven into the foundational framework of how you live.

It is also an invitation into a community committed to personal and collective spiritual development — to sitting with the Great Teachers, and to implementing what we receive and learn out into the world.

If that is a current you would like to stay close to, you can join below, or simply reply.
