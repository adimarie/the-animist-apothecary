A Word First

This community grows by relational fabric

Not by cold finding. Not by ad pixel or feed scroll. Each newcomer enters the way old gatherings have always opened — through a name, through a relationship, through a thread of trust that someone is willing to carry on your behalf.

It is how the village remembers itself. It is how the elder houses kept their gates — not closed, but tended.

So if you are arriving here, you are arriving with someone in mind, or someone has put this in your hands. Either way: welcome. The door is here, and it is open.

The Monthly Newcomers Q&A

An open hour, once a month

We hold this gathering on a monthly rhythm — an unhurried hour for the people arriving new to this work. To meet me. To meet a few others walking the same threshold. To ask the questions that brought you here, and to be met where you are.

·

What it is

An open hour of meeting — not a sales call, not a class. A gathering at the threshold for the people considering walking further in.

· ·

What you bring

Your name. The name of the person who sent you. The questions you are carrying. A willingness to be seen and to listen.

· · ·

What you leave with

A clearer sense of whether this work is for you — and a place in the seasonal letter where the next gatherings live.

Dates and times are announced through the seasonal letter as each one approaches. Registration below holds your place on the list.
