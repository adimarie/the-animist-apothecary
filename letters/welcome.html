A Word First

A practice taking new shape

For many years, this work has been held in close circles — in private ceremony, in one-on-one conversation, in the quiet trust of those who have walked alongside me.

The Animist Apothecary is the formal home of that practice now. A named threshold for what has long been forming. As the seasons turn and the next year of gatherings begins to take shape, I am opening up the seasonal letter — the regular dispatch of this practice — to the people I have shared this work with.

That includes you.

What lives inside the letter

Training Grounds

Ongoing courses for those deepening into their skills, their gifts, and the practice of embodying what you bring to and from the ceremony room — out into this world.

Travel & Retreats

The journeys ahead — the deeper containers held in sacred geographies. Where the work goes wide-open under sky and over ocean and into the bones of older lands.

The Deeper Question

How do we cultivate
an Entheogenically Awakened Society
from within the heart of our own lives?

From within our own communities. From within our own homes. Woven into the overarching currents and foundational framework of how we choose to live.

How does one enliven, embolden, and empower their own awakening into the work of their life — and into the lives of the people they hold?

That is the question we are tending here.

What you are part of

A community committed to personal
and collective spiritual development

To sitting with the Great Teachers. To receiving what they offer. And to implementing what we receive and learn out into the world — into our work, our relationships, our households, our places.

The letter is the channel that keeps this current alive between us. Less an events calendar than a way of staying close — to the work, to the season, to one another.

Bringing Someone In

An invitation arrives through someone

This community grows through relational entry — not through cold finding. If you know someone whose presence would tend this work, send them the door, or write me directly.

New folks enter through the Monthly Newcomers Q&A — an open hour to meet, to ask, to be met. Attendance is by reference, so each newcomer arrives with a name and a relationship.
