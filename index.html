Welcome to an eternal edge of infinite horizons

After 16 years of service as a Somatic Touch Therapist and Hands-on Spiritual Practitioner — my work through Bodywork & Botanicals has come into a new evolution of specialized offerings and curation.

The many people who came seeking respite on the massage table almost always had deeper wounds that needed attending to — expressing as physical symptoms of disharmony within their system as pain.

After over a decade of devoted study, initiation, and maturation as an answer to the deeper calling of my gifts in service to the world, I now have a richer set of specialized skills to provide the much needed deep work to bring about life changing contact with the inner domains of Spirit and Self.

What has always been here remains: deep somatic tending, sacred space, relational care, and the devoted art of helping people return to themselves. And something new is arriving, too.

Your Practitioner

Adi Marie

Interdimensional Intermediary & Guide Somatic Medium & Oracular Diviner Ceremonial & Ritual Stewardship

This work is based in Southern California with a regular migratory route along the North American West Coast. Adi Marie is flown around the world serving individuals and communities ready to embark upon and enact great change — first and foremost within themselves, to then do so to the world around.

Adi has always had an immense power and gift in the spoken word, serving as an oracular poet, mythmaker, and storyteller — synthesizing and cohering the messages from the otherworlds into a tapestry of tales for us to live within. This gift has been formalized through her initiation as a voice diviner, having been chosen for the role by the Spirit World.

Her gifts of somatic mediumship led her into medical, therapeutic, and shamanic bodywork, providing deep spiritual and emotional openings for physicalization and somatic expression and embodiment of the psyche and soul. Adi Marie has over 19 years of experience in the world of medicines of the wilds, specializing in entheogenic sacraments — guiding others through specialized and extraordinary states of visionary consciousness.

For those whose inner work moves outward — and impacts thousands of lives — whose healing shapes collective fields of community and industry — for the innovators, the creators, and the world shapers, this work is for you.

Adi Marie works with visionary leaders and masters of craft — those passionate in their disciplines, ready to step deeper into the mysteries of the ineffable within their inner worlds, and to plunge the depths to bring more light to the worlds we share.

She also works with established communities to further their healing — so that those who are ready may begin to step into their leadership and elderhood, forming nutritive and nourishing ecosystems within which the children, the troubled, the lost, and the youth may heal and be held, thrive and grow, and bring forth hope for the restoration of the village amidst the urban sprawl.

She helps those ready to hone their burgeoning spiritual gifts into formidable powers — to be wielded benevolently in the shaping of a more beautiful world we all know is possible.
