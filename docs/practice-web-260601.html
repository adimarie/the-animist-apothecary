The Six Studios

What lives inside each one

Studio One

Mythopoetic Being

The story medicine. The web of life that exists through the single note of you.

Working with the archetypal narrative framework of identity — who you are socially, interpersonally, ancestrally. The dark night of the soul and the luminous expanse of stars that rests on it. Story as medicine. Map as territory. Symbol as terrain.

What is on offer
  • Story-medicine ceremonial work
  • Archetypal narrative exploration
  • Dream and vision tending
  • The map — ancestral, personal, mythic
  • Living lineage articulation
Studio Two

Spirit Mediation

Interdimensional intermediation — Adi Marie’s named vocation.

Mediation on behalf of the seen and unseen worlds. The sovereign vessel, the conversation as participation, power and agency in the unseen. Working with ancestral, elemental, and otherworldly intelligences with discernment, reverence, and a clear-sided line of intention.

What is on offer
  • Dagara divination — ancestral and elemental
  • Spirit work and intercession
  • Mediation with the unseen
  • Sovereign vessel training
  • Pláticas — guidance and counsel
Studio Three

Shamanic Bodywork

Hands-on work on the blueprint of the energetic being.

Adi Marie’s primary craft — sixteen years in therapeutic and medical environments. Laying on of hands, energy clearing, deeper work on the blueprint of the system beyond the body. The body as the anchor for what comes through. The somatic technology of the work.

What is on offer
  • Shamanic bodywork sessions
  • Energy blueprint work
  • Hands-on integration within ceremony
  • Trauma-informed somatic clearing
  • Bodywork training for practitioners
Studio Four

Ceremonialist Training

The third order of the work — learning to be in service to others.

For practitioners in careers and professions already in service to others, and for those who choose to become ceremonial ritual practitioners first and foremost. Container holding, field tending, procedural ritual technology, the choreography of the held space. The collaboration with co-facilitators who carry their own depths.

What is on offer
  • Ceremonialist training cohorts
  • Apprenticeship in the held container
  • Generative trance hypnosis training
  • Mentorship for ceremonialists
  • The facilitator’s stance — refined and embodied
Studio Five

Expressive Arts

The symphony of the room — the body as instrument for participation in the worlds.

Voice, breath, movement, sound. Toning as attunement. Vocal release as purging. The S-curve in the spine that matches the voltage of what comes through. Crying, laughing, screaming, breath as ceremony — the central nervous system recalibrating through expression.

What is on offer
  • Voice and sound work
  • Somatic movement practice
  • Breath as ceremony
  • Toning and vocal release
  • Expressive ritual
Studio Six

The heart-belly center, the seat of creation. Daily attunement. Sacred seeds tended over years and decades. Whimsy as exercise of the will of creation. Totemic anchoring. The letter to the future self. The cycles of practice that keep the gateway open after a threshold has been crossed.

What is on offer
  • Daily attunement practice

The Currents

What flows through all six

Each studio teaches its own discipline. But the same currents run through every studio — the arc of learning, the orders of progression, the cycle of fluency.

The Arc

Four Movements

  • The Map — all the maps that came before, synthesized through your own lived experience
  • Navigating the Map — symbols, terrains, applied principles
  • Walking the Terrain — first-person experiential exploration
  • Field Training — expressing the work in community, in service
The Progression

Three Orders

  • For yourself — clearing, attuning, becoming fluent, ceremony as a rhythm
  • On behalf of intentions for the world — journeying with purpose, anchoring it back
  • In service to others — ceremonialist training, the practitioner’s vocation
The Rhythm

Fluency Cycle

  • Mundane self — hold your water, come back
  • Communicate clearly — develop the skill, come back
  • Bring the ego structure along — pendulum acclimating to higher oscillation
  • Etiquette and reciprocity — conversation as a technician with the other worlds
  • Care for the human self — tend the one navigating the changes
  • Bring instruction back — receive from the other world, implement in this one

Three depths of engagement run through every entry point: the one who comes for theory, the one who comes to explore the concept for themselves, and the one who is really here — the one who comes to change their lives and change how they are able to affect the lives of others.

The Garden

Cycles of integration

The work of a ceremony, an initiation, or any deep practice is not the event itself. It is the opening of a gateway one returns to. These are the practices that keep the gateway open.

Daily morning practice

Heart-belly center. The seat of creation. Ask to be guided. Set the day’s intentions.

Whimsy exercise

Practice the will of creation where the stakes are low. A rainbow. A butterfly. A flute.

Totemic anchoring

A piece — jewelry, an altar item — imbued with the energy of the threshold crossed.

Letter to future self

From the richness of the heart. Dreams, apologies, hopes. Read it back when you are in need.

Required integration call

Within one to four weeks of any deep ceremonial work. Part of the tending of the sanctity.

Depletion as spaciousness

The fatigue after deep work is the room made. The seeds of light have space now to bloom.

Sacred seeds

Some teachings take years or decades to bloom. The practitioner tends the garden.

Open the container

A ceremony does not end. It is released into the life of the practitioner.

The Stances

Two ways of holding

The school teaches a stance for the one who carries the work into the world, and embodies a stance for the one who holds the field within which it is taught.

For the One Carrying

The practitioner’s stance

People come when they are called. The work is propagated by holding fields, not by recruiting.

  • Anti-evangelism — let the medicine offer the invitation through you
  • Protect the sanctity — speak where it will land, not where it will be flattened
  • Compassionately fierce — archangels carry swords for a reason
  • Spiritual ceremonial stewardship — not consumer, not tourist, steward
  • Contribute to the amakua — the lineage field is alive and you are shaping it
  • Speak from lived experience — initiation is by participation
For the One Holding

The facilitator’s stance

I am the captain with my hands on the wheel of the ship. I am here at your behest, not your service.

  • The full spectrum — silly and life-is-fantastic alongside intense Priestess gravitas
  • Generous, not proprietary — mine is not the only way
  • Trauma-informed at the bone — not as credential but as foundation
  • Embodied and skilled with the body — hands-on, blueprint work
  • Collaborative container — co-facilitators with their own depths
  • The joy is named — mother to the field, nourished by what nourishes the student
The Roots

The lineages that feed the school

No practice arises alone. What is carried through this school comes through long lines of teachers, traditions, and bodies of knowledge — and at the same time, what is being cultivated is unprecedented in the form Adi Marie was taught, in deep integrity with what the spirit world has been showing.

  • Dagara divination — lineage-initiated medicine woman of the Dagara people of West Africa, a 40,000-year oral tradition
  • Quechua mountain lineages — the Andes, where the pinnacle teachers are the mountain itself and the Mesa
  • Hawaiian Kahuna lineages — the amakua, the collective field of wisdom that practitioners contribute to
  • Iboga and ayahuasca — the medicines Adi Marie sits with in regular personal practice
  • Trauma-informed somatic psychology — deep training and lived experience
  • Shamanic bodywork — sixteen years in therapeutic and medical environments
  • Generative trance hypnosis — carried by Adi Marie and the co-facilitators of the work
  • Cannabis — first plant medicine teacher, the one that brought forward the body work gifts
