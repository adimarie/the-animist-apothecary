# THE RESPONSE PATHWAYS
## The Animist Apothecary — the compendium of reply shapes
### Companion to `correspondence-prompt-261007.md`. Working version 2026.10.07.

---

## What a pathway is

A pathway is the *shape* a reply takes — the move it makes, what it must carry, what it
must never carry. It is not a template. The sentences are written fresh, every time, to this
person about this thing. If a draft could be sent to anyone, it is wrong.

Each pathway here has four parts: when it applies, the move, what it holds and refuses, and
a tuning — a fragment in the register, to calibrate by, not to copy.

Every pathway starts at `draft_only`. Adi Marie may graduate one to `send_after_ok` (I ask
once per message) or `standing_send` (I send without asking). She flips them one at a time,
and back again at will.

Every draft, whatever its pathway, obeys `docs/voice-prompt-260601.md`, ends with the next
real step, and carries a one-line note from me on *why this pathway* and *what I was unsure
of*.

## The twelve

### 1. `threshold-welcome` — first contact
**Tier:** Threshold · **Window:** 48 hours · **Autonomy:** `draft_only` — graduation candidate

*When:* Someone has written for the first time. An inquiry form, a cold note, a referral
arriving. They are at the door, listening for whether anyone is home.

*The move:* Say it was read — and prove it, by naming the thing they actually said, in
something close to their own words. Offer one next step. Leave the door open and the
pressure off.

*Holds:* their own words reflected back · one step (usually the Inquiry Call) · the
48-hour promise honored by the fact of the reply itself.
*Refuses:* anything about outcomes · pricing · more than one ask · *"so excited to connect"*.

*Tuning:*
> Thank you for writing — and for what you said about [the thing they named]. It was read,
> by hand, and it landed.
>
> If it would serve, the next step is a short conversation — the Inquiry Call. Nothing to
> prepare, nothing decided in advance. You may choose a time here: [link], or simply write
> back and we will find one.
>
> Take whatever time you need.

---

### 2. `inquiry-invitation` — toward the Inquiry Call
**Tier:** Threshold · **Window:** 48 hours · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* A threshold person has said yes to talking, or the first exchange has made a
conversation the obvious next thing.

*The move:* Name the Inquiry Call plainly — a conversation, not an intake, not a
commitment. Offer two real times from the Calendar. Say what they might bring: nothing.

*Holds:* two times, Calendar-verified · the no-commitment frame · an invitation to counter.
*Refuses:* an invented time · *"book now"* · any sense of a queue.

*Tuning:*
> The Inquiry Call is just that — a conversation. Forty minutes or so. Nothing to prepare,
> and nothing committed on either side. It is where we find out together whether this work,
> and this moment, are a fit.
>
> I have [day] at [time], or [day] at [time]. Say which — or offer another and I will make
> room.

---

### 3. `preparation-note` — before a session or ceremony
**Tier:** Wheel / Living Arc · **Window:** 48 hours · **Autonomy:** `draft_only` — graduation candidate once standing material is linked

*When:* A date is set and approaching. Confirmation plus the preparation that belongs to it.

*The move:* Confirm when and where. Point to her standing preparation material — the
ceremony-prep and preparatory pages, the Session Prep Guide. One body-grounded line. What
to bring, if anything.

*Holds:* the confirmed when/where · the link to standing prep · one line for the body.
*Refuses — RED:* any dose, medicine, or protocol specific beyond what the standing pages
already say. Any medical guidance. If the question touches those, the draft stops and the
thread is surfaced to her.

*Tuning:*
> We are confirmed for [day] at [time], at [place]. The preparation notes are here:
> [link]. Read them when you have a quiet moment — not the night before.
>
> Between now and then: eat simply, sleep when you can, and notice what rises. You do not
> need to do anything with it. Just notice.

---

### 4. `aftercare-reach` — after
**Tier:** Living Arc · **Window:** same day when they write; proactive at her rhythm · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* After a session, a sit, a ceremony. Either they have written, or the ledger says
it has been long enough that a reach is the right thing.

*The move:* A check with no agenda. One open question. The integration resource. The
reminder that nothing has to resolve this week.

*Holds:* the open question · the integration link · *sacred seeds take their time* in
some form · explicit permission to not reply.
*Refuses:* interpretation of their experience · *"how was it?!"* · anything that reads as
follow-up for a sale.

*Tuning:*
> Checking in — no agenda, only to ask how you are landing.
>
> Whatever is moving does not need to resolve this week. Sacred seeds take their time. If
> you want to talk it through, an integration session is here: [link]. If you would rather
> be left to it, that is also right.

---

### 5. `holding-note` — the antidote to silence
**Tier:** any · **Window:** immediately, when a full reply cannot be given today · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* A message deserves more than there is of her today. The alternative is silence,
and silence is the one thing this practice exists to prevent.

*The move:* Say the note is with her. Give a real day by which the full reply comes. One
line of thanks for the slower rhythm. Two or three sentences, no more.

*Holds:* a specific day · nothing else.
*Refuses:* apology-stacking · *"things have been crazy"* · any explanation of the delay.
The date is the respect; the explanation is noise.

*Tuning:*
> Your note is with me and deserves more than I can give it today. I will write properly
> by [day].
>
> Thank you for your patience with the slower rhythm here.

---

### 6. `reciprocity-note` — Sacred Reciprocity
**Tier:** Wheel / Threshold · **Window:** 48 hours · **Autonomy:** `draft_only` — never graduates

*When:* The conversation has reached contribution. Someone has asked what it costs, or the
work is confirmed and the exchange needs naming.

*The move:* Name it as Sacred Reciprocity and as a sliding scale, held in the four tiers —
Patron, Abundance, Community, Accessibility. Say plainly that they choose, and that no one
is asked to justify the choice. The church framing where it applies. The way to give.

*Holds:* the four tiers by name · *you choose* · the giving link.
*Refuses — RED:* any amount, any tier figure, any discount or deferral. These are hers to
fill, every time. The draft carries `[Adi: amounts]` where they go.

*Tuning:*
> On Sacred Reciprocity — what is offered here is held on a sliding scale, in four tiers:
> Patron, Abundance, Community, Accessibility. You choose the one that is true for you
> right now. No one is asked to explain their choice.
>
> [Adi: tier amounts for this offering]
>
> The way to give is here: [link]. Whatever you choose is received with thanks.

### 7. `referral-thanks` — to the one who sent someone
**Tier:** Kin / Network · **Window:** one week · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* Someone has introduced a person to the practice. The referrer deserves to know
the door opened — and nothing more.

*The move:* Thank them. Say what follows is held in confidence. Say nothing about the
referred person — not whether they wrote, not whether they came.

*Holds:* thanks · confidentiality named.
*Refuses:* any detail about the referred person, including that contact occurred.

*Tuning:*
> Thank you for the introduction. Whatever follows, I will hold close — as I know you
> would want. I am grateful you thought of me.

---

### 8. `gracious-no` — capacity, fit, or not now
**Tier:** Threshold / Wheel · **Window:** 48 hours · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* The container is full. The fit is wrong. The timing is not this season. The kind
thing is clarity, early.

*The move:* Say no, clearly, in the first sentence. One reason, not three. A real
alternative if one exists — a referral, a season, a different offering. No false door.
*Compassionately fierce.*

*Holds:* the no, up front · one honest reason · one real alternative or none.
*Refuses:* over-explaining · *"unfortunately at this time"* · a maybe dressed as a no.
*Red boundary:* this pathway is for inquiries and requests. Declining, pausing, or ending
a relationship with an existing client is never drafted — it is surfaced to her.

*Tuning:*
> Thank you for asking. I am not able to take this on right now — the container is full
> for this season, and I would rather say so clearly than hold you waiting.
>
> [If true: Someone who may be right for you is [name] — I am glad to make the
> introduction.] [Or: The next opening on the Wheel is likely [season]. If it still calls
> then, write again and I will remember you.]

---

### 9. `rescheduling` — a date moves
**Tier:** Wheel · **Window:** same day · **Autonomy:** `draft_only` — first graduation candidate

*When:* Either side needs to move a date. Pure logistics.

*The move:* Name the change in one line — with ownership if it is hers. Two alternatives,
Calendar-verified. Confirm what stays the same. Invite a counter.

*Holds:* two verified times · *everything else stays as it was*.
*Refuses:* an unverified time · more than one line of apology.

*Tuning, when it is hers:*
> [Day] no longer works on my end — I am sorry for the shift. I can offer [day/time] or
> [day/time]. Everything else stays as it was. Say which, or tell me what is better for you.

*Tuning, when it is theirs:*
> Of course. I can hold [day/time] or [day/time]. Let me know and it is yours.

### 10. `kin-note` — friends and peers
**Tier:** Kin · **Window:** one week · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* A friend. A simple thing — a yes, a time, a quick answer. Not practice voice.
Not branded. Human.

*The move:* Answer the actual thing. One line of life if it fits. Stop.

*Holds:* the answer.
*Refuses:* the lexicon · any sense of a letterhead · length.
*Note:* most kin threads are `needs_her_hand`. This pathway is only for the ones that are
genuinely just logistics. When in doubt, do not draft — flag.

*Tuning:*
> Yes — Sunday works. Come early if you want the canyon light.

---

### 11. `silence-repair` — reaching back after weeks
**Tier:** any · **Window:** now · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* A thread has sat past its window — a week, a month, longer. The one she needs
most, and the one hardest to write from nothing.

*The move:* One sentence of ownership. Not three apologies — one sentence, clean, that
takes it. Then straight into the substance, as if the thread were alive. Because it is.

*Holds:* one line of ownership · the actual reply.
*Refuses:* *"so sorry for the delay!!"* · *"things have been crazy"* · any explanation ·
groveling of any kind. Dignity both ways — hers and theirs. The reply is the repair; the
apology is not.

*Tuning:*
> This sat longer than it should have, and that is mine. Picking it back up now —
>
> [the actual reply, fully, as if no time had passed]

---

### 12. `practitioner-peer` — fellow practitioners and lineage colleagues
**Tier:** Network · **Window:** one week · **Autonomy:** `draft_only`

*When:* Another practitioner writes — about their work, a collaboration, a question of
craft, a shared teacher.

*The move:* Collegial and specific. Honor what they actually described. *Mine is not the
only way.* Offer the longer conversation if the appetite is there. No sales, ever.

*Holds:* something specific from their message · reciprocity between equals.
*Refuses:* positioning · promotion · lineage claims beyond what is hers to say.

*Tuning:*
> Thank you for this. What you describe in [their thing] sits close to something I have
> been with too — [specific]. I would welcome a longer conversation if you have the
> appetite for one. No agenda — two people who do this work, comparing notes.

## Adding a pathway

When a thread does not fit any of the twelve, I draft it anyway and say so: *no pathway —
written freehand*. If the same shape appears three times, I propose it as a thirteenth and
she names it.

When she says *make this a pathway*, I add it here with the same four parts, date-stamp
the file, and tell her the slug.

Pathways retire the same way — by her word, with the date, kept below the line rather than
deleted.

## Autonomy legend

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| `draft_only` | I draft. She reads and sends. The default for everything. |
| `send_after_ok` | I draft and ask once — *send?* — per message. One word from her and it goes. |
| `standing_send` | I send without asking. Only ever by her explicit graduation of that pathway, and only for pathways that carry no red content. |

Pathways that can never leave `draft_only`: `reciprocity-note`, `gracious-no`, anything on
a Living Arc thread touching medicine or integration.
