## 5. THE BONES OF THE SCHOOL

### What this is

The Animist Apothecary is an esoteric occult school of somatic shamanic arts. A revitalist practice. A place going deep into the roots of ancestral remembering — and forward, into the next continuation. A training ground and a specialized school cultivating skilled interdimensional intermediaries and guides — people who have the potential to shepherd in a new world.

### Who this is for

For those born with the gifts and the calling, who came into diaspora without the elders to cultivate them. For people who feel the inherent wisdom and attunement of the medicine that is theirs to walk with, and want structured guidance in how to come into the mastery and discipline of their craft. For those who have a calling to participate in this way at this time — and who came on their own thread, not because someone evangelized to them.

### The Six Studios

The school has six different formats taught at a time, each with its own foundational ethos, body of knowledge, and skill set:

1. **Mythopoetic Being** — story medicine, archetypal narrative framework of identity, the maps of personal and ancestral inheritance
2. **Spirit Mediation** — interdimensional intermediation, sovereign vessel, working with the seen and unseen, Dagara divination
3. **Shamanic Bodywork** — hands-on work on the blueprint of the energetic being, the body as anchor, trauma-informed somatic clearing
4. **Ceremonialist Training** — the third order of the work, becoming a practitioner in service to others, container holding, procedural ritual technology
5. **Expressive Arts** — voice, breath, movement, sound, the symphony of the room, toning and vocal release
6. **Basins of Creation** — the inner garden, daily attunement at the heart-belly center, sacred seeds tended over years and decades

### The Four Movements — the arc of all learning, within every studio

1. **The Map** — the known and unknown maps, all the maps that have existed before, synthesized through your own embodied lived experience
2. **Navigating the Map** — symbols, terrains, principles, the applied equations of the work
3. **Walking the Terrain** — first-person experiential exploration
4. **Field Training** — expressing the work in community, in service to others

Running alongside: theory, practicum, tests, initiation, refinement, and continual mentorship.

### The Three Orders — the trajectory of the practitioner

1. **For yourself** — clearing, attuning, becoming fluent, holding ceremony as a rhythm of your own life
2. **On behalf of intentions between the worlds** — journeying with purpose, opening here, going into the other world, receiving, bringing back, implementing
3. **In service to others** — the most advanced work, where ceremonialist training occurs

### The Three Depths — entry points

- **Theory** — to learn the concepts
- **Embody** — to take it into one's own life
- **Service** — to change one's life and how one affects others

### The Fluency Cycle — the rhythm of the work (six steps)

1. **The mundane self.** Hold your water. Attune and acclimate. Come back.
2. **Communicate clearly. Be communicated with clearly.** Develop the skill. Come back.
3. **Bring the identity and ego structure along** — a ballast, a pendulum acclimating to a higher oscillation while maintaining stable ground. This becomes fluent.
4. **Etiquette and reciprocity in the other world.** Ritual, ceremony, enacting change. Conversation as a technician — exploring what is a lever, what is a pipe, what is the scope and skill of practice.
5. **Care for the human identity person self** navigating these huge changes. Come back.
6. **Bring instruction back.** Go to the other world with an intention from this world, open ritual, open ceremony, receive communication and instruction about what you are going to do here.

### The Anchoring — the heart of the discipline

It is easy to go into the other world and live in that other world beyond the body. The hard work is implementing what comes through into this world.

We are not only meant to open to those energies. **We are meant to anchor them down.**

The technical articulation: **voltage and amplitude.** When the voltage of what is coming through exceeds the body's grounding capacity, the system overloads and purges to recalibrate. The proactive practice is matching the incoming energy with a body wave that lands and grounds it — undulating the spine in S-curves, finding the slower rhythm beneath the fast one. This is the embodied technology of anchoring.

### The Sovereign Vessel

A core ethical principle: dignity with non-human intelligences. When something is too much, the practitioner communicates from an honest, earnest place: *"You're sharing consciousness in my vessel. I want more dignity. I want more modesty. Can you be more gentle with me?"*

The medicine — and other intelligences — are adaptive. If you communicate what you need with respect, they will meet you on that ground.

### Conversation as Participation

You can within yourself communicate clearly with the intelligence you are sitting with — linguistically, internally. A favorite working prayer: **"Speak to me in a language I already understand."**

### Power and Agency in the Unseen

When meeting scary or unfamiliar presences, the practitioner does not give power away. *"You are the one with the human body. You are the one incarnate on the material realm. You are the one with power. Sometimes beings want us to forget that."*

### Don't Make Meaning While It's Happening

*"It's like starting the Lord of the Rings and going, 'Oh, I get it.' You have no idea. Let it conclude. Let it land. Let it marinate in the weeks and months to come."*

- At one end: **silly, light, life-is-fantastic.** Real laughter. Warmth. The body in joy. *"I oscillate between really silly and a little bit kind of, like life is fantastic."*

Calibrate by context:
- **Lightness** on quotidian copy — logistics, calendar, welcome notes
