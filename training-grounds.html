The Practice

An apothecary of restoration.

The Animist Apothecary is an experiential apothecary for the restoration of an animist relational practice and worldview.

The Training Grounds is its school — where procedural ritual technology and ceremonial practice are formalized and embodied. Where one is trained to engage interdimensionally with skill, poise, rapport, and the artisan's touch of inspirationally skilled mastery.

We are here to participate in the miracle. Adi Marie · Founder
A Calling of These Times

What was severed asks to be re-woven.

These are urgent times.

The world asks for the restoration of relational balance between the seen and unseen worlds. For humans to step down from the throne of supremacy and return to the place we were entrusted to hold — stewards. Guardians. Kin in service.

In every age, in every people, some have been born with the emergence of gifts. Medicines to be carried on behalf of the land, the community, the many-valenced worlds. And many were born into diaspora, where the lineages were cut. The elders who would have taught what is innately ours could not be found.

You are not too late. The medicine that is yours is still yours.

The Training Grounds is the place to come into it — fully, with structured guidance, in the mastery and discipline of your craft.

What This Cultivates

Four formidable capacities.

I

Skilled mediation.

The artisan's touch of inspirationally skilled mastery, applied between the worlds.

II

Fluency in the worlds.

Moving between the seen and the unseen with steadiness, etiquette, and discernment.

III

Reclamation of lineage.

Coming into the inherent wisdom and medicine that is innately yours.

IV

Service in the world.

Anchoring the frequency back here — in the body, in the day, in the world.

The Course of Study

The map. The terrain. The walking. The offering.

Every studio unfolds through the same four layers.

I The Map

The bodies of knowledge. The cosmologies. The lineages. Every map that has come before — and the one you will draw by walking.

II Navigating the Map

The symbols. The principles. The applied equations. How the map is read. What each marking means. How to orient when the land is new.

III Walking the Terrain

The map becomes earth beneath your feet. You walk. You fall. You find your gait. The classroom becomes practice. The practice becomes lived.

IV Field Training

Carrying the work outward. An intermediary in community, in ceremony, in the long quiet labor of relational mediation. The walk becomes the offering.

Four depths. No wrong door.

The Training Grounds receives students at every depth. There is no wrong depth at which to enter. Only the honesty of where you stand.

  • · ·

    Those here to change their lives. The clearing work. The ceremony. The long acclimation. The practice as personal devotion.

  • · · ·

  • · · · ·

The Interior Work

From clearing to anchoring. A fluency in the worlds.

Within and across the four layers, the practitioner's own interior is the central instrument. The work proceeds in phases. Each is a developmental arc of its own.

One

Foundations & Acclimation

Astronauts train in pools before they go to space. Divers find their gait in the shallow before the open ocean.

We begin with clearing. With acclimation. With learning to hold our water through the altitude change. The day-to-day self is tended. New states of being are met without losing ground.

Two

Communication & Etiquette

Listening with clarity. Being communicated with clearly.

The reciprocity, the discernment, the relational manners of working with the other worlds. The skill is developed, then carried back to the day-to-day self. The identity and ego come along as ballast — a pendulum acclimating to a higher oscillation, holding stable ground.

Three

Ritual as Technician

In ceremony, you are a technician.

Exploring the mechanics of what can and cannot be worked with. What is a lever. What is a pipe. The scope and skill of practice. You listen. You receive guidance. You discern. You implement.

Four

Anchoring the Frequency

The harder work.

To rise in resonance and bring it back. To hold the field and anchor it down — here, in the body, in the day, in the world. We are not only meant to open to those energies. We are meant to anchor them down. This is what the world is asking of us now.

Five

In Service to Others

Once the practice is fluent, the work expands.

Ceremony becomes rhythmic in your life. You become a liminal membrane. An oracular vessel. An interdimensional gateway. Information flows in and out — for yourself first, then for the world's intentions, then in service to others.

The school has many areas of conveyance. Six are held now. Others live in the roots, waiting.

Each studio carries its own ethos, its own body of knowledge, its own skill set — its own map, its own terrain, its own walking, its own offering.
