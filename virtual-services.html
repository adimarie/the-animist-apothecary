Sacred Reciprocity

Each offering is held on a sliding scale. The tiers are an invitation to give from where you truly are — the higher tiers help hold space for those who give from the lower ones.

Reciprocity is sent by Venmo once your hour is held. Your place is confirmed by hand once it arrives.

If something shifts

Intake & Inquiry and Guidance & Counsel — reschedule up to 48 hours before; cancel with 7 days’ notice.

Oracular & Elemental Divination — reschedule up to 7 days before; cancel with 14 days’ notice.

You’ll receive a link to manage your booking in your confirmation email.
