A Closed Ceremonial Community

The Kinship Nexus

A place to grow in our depth, together — a web of kin gathered around one hearth, participating in growing an entheogenically awakened society.

Some work cannot happen in public. Not because it is secret, but because it is intimate — the kind of depth that only grows where the same faces return, season after season, to the same fire.

The Premise

A community in ceremony.

The Kinship Nexus is a sanctuary of integrity — a closed ceremonial community devoted to personal and collective transformation through intentional community, sacred practice, and ceremony. It is not a series of events you attend, but a body you belong to: the long fire of the Apothecary, an ongoing fellowship of practitioners, seekers, and stewards who have chosen to walk together over years rather than evenings.

Its premise is simple and vast: participating in growing an entheogenically awakened society. We gather to sit with our otherworldly teachers, and to bring what they show us back into our daily lives — to healthfully shape how we live and play and create and love. Here we cultivate deep relational bonds, ceremonial literacy, ecological reverence, and the courage to live in alignment with our highest nature.

Entering this space, we make promises to one another — of mutual respect and benevolence. We create this world together.

The Name, In Two Halves

Kinship, and the place it crosses.

Kinship

The web of relation

Kinship here means more than fellowship among humans. It is the animist understanding that land, lineage, and the more-than-human world are relatives — and that a community in right relationship tends all of them. Within the circle, it means being genuinely known: not the polished self, but the becoming one, witnessed over years by people who have earned the seeing.

Nexus

The meeting place

A nexus is where the threads cross. This is the hearth the community gathers around — the ceremonies, the seasonal convenings, the shared practice that binds solitary paths into one weave. What each member carries alone between gatherings is brought back to the crossing point, where it is witnessed, tempered, and sent out again.

Who Gathers Here

The company of the fire.

Many of the Nexus’ members are high-level visionaries and innovators — people whose work in the world has brought goodness to the lives of many others by changing systems and cultures. Here, they set the public role down and are met as kin.

This is a place to gather and be inspired — by the teachers of the otherworlds, and by one another. What each member receives alone becomes, at this fire, something the whole circle grows by.

Why the Door is Kept

Closed, so it can be deep.

The door is not closed to exclude. It is closed the way a vessel is closed — because what happens inside needs walls to happen at all. Ceremony of real depth asks for continuity, confidentiality, and trust that has been built rather than assumed. A circle whose faces change every month can be warm; it cannot be deep.

So entry is guarded by a stringent screening — the promise the community makes to everyone already inside it: each person at the fire has been met, oriented, and found ready. Safety is the first hospitality.

What We Cultivate Together

The life of the circle.

Membership opens into a wide weave of offerings held through the turning year — from lighthearted outings to the deepest immersive work — each one yours to opt into as you feel called.

  • Ceremonial spaces & gatherings. Regular ceremonial sits in a safe, held space, guided by experienced facilitators.
  • Weekly integration circles. Ongoing support to process, share, and embody insights.
  • Seasonal immersions. Multi-day experiences to deepen our connection to self, community, and the land.
  • Teachings & workshops. Ceremonial literacy, somatic practice, shadow work, ecology, ancestral traditions, and more.
  • Land stewardship. Hands-on care for the land that nourishes us.
  • A private community channel. Where gatherings, retreats, ceremonies, and outings are shared throughout the year, to join as you feel called.
  • Library & resource access. Curated books, teachings, and tools to support your path.
  • Accountability & support. We walk beside each other in our growth and commitment to the work.

The river taught that its waters belong to every shore it touches.

Our Agreements

The bones of the circle.

The Nexus is held by agreements, not expectations. These are the bones of the container — the same for everyone inside it.

  • Confidentiality is sacred. What lives inside the container stays inside the container. The stories of the circle are treated as holy ground.
  • Do your own work. The circle witnesses and strengthens; it never replaces. Each member tends their own becoming.
  • Speak from truth. Come from love as you express yourself — even when what you carry is hard, messy, or raw.
  • No fixing, no rescuing. Each member remains the sovereign authority of their own life and path.
  • Show up, be present. Membership is a sustained devotion, not a drop-in. The circle’s depth is made of its members’ returning.
  • Honor the medicine, the land, and each other. The kinship extends beyond the human — to lineage, to place, and to the more-than-human kin the ceremonies serve.

As within, so throughout. By healing ourselves, we help heal the land, our communities, and the generations to come. Together, we remember.

The Way In

An invitational path.

The Kinship Nexus is an invitational community, and the way in is walked slowly — four movements, each one a deepening of the conversation.

01

Reach Out

Send a message expressing your interest and a little about yourself — what calls you, and what you carry.

02

Application & Deep Inquiry

A written application of real depth — personal reflections, history, and intention. This is where the conversation truly begins.

03

The Conversation

A stringent screening and a genuine conversation — to ensure the Nexus is right for you, that you are ready for the commitment, and that safety is held for all.

04

Invitation & Initiation

If aligned, you are offered a place in the community, welcomed with a one-time initiation contribution, and brought to the fire.

Upon initiation, members join the private community channel — a living calendar of ceremonies, gatherings, retreats, and outings, answered as you feel called. And if the Nexus is not the right fit at this time, you are not turned away: the public community welcomes you, and the relationship keeps growing at its own pace.

Not everyone is called. Not everyone is ready. But if you are — we would be honored to walk this path with you.
