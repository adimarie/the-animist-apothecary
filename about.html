Our Mission

The Animist Apothecary exists within an animist frame, and explores, exercises, and expresses itself as such.

Awakening more fully to the ever-present beauty of creation upon this world.

We are here for the restoration of balance and peace. We serve as emissaries and intermediaries and, at our very best, as honorable interdimensional diplomats.

Humanity is preparing to emerge onto the planes of galactic consciousness, and it is imperative that we assume the dignity of the responsibilities that accompany such work, especially at such times. We begin with the respectful, restorative responsibility of our inheritance of this Earth, and of our many, many, many other-than-human, at times non-corporeal, Kin.

What does it mean to emerge as an entheogenically awakened society?

This is the premise of the School, of the Foundation, and of the Ministry.

I · What We Hold True

The principles of the Apothecary

The Animist Apothecary rests on an understanding older than any of its offerings — older than the hands that tend it. The Earth is alive and sovereign. The cosmos and the stars, the plants and the animals, the waters, stones, mountains, winds and fire, the ancestors and the elemental forces — all carry personhood, agency, and sacredness. We do not treat the world as a resource. We know it as a relative.

From that knowing, everything else follows. The spiritual life is fundamentally relational — a continuous, ceremonial direct-relationship with the living forces of creation, in which communion is real, mutual, and reciprocal. Ceremony is how that relationship is made tangible. Not metaphor. Practice.

  • The world is kin. All beings carry spirit, and the work of healing is the restoration of relationship between a person and the living world they belong to.
  • The ancestors remain. The dead are agentive and continuous with the living. We are held within an ancestral field — and tending that line, through offerings, altar work, divination, and ceremony, is a sacred obligation. Unattended ancestral grief produces suffering in the living.
  • The tradition is oral. This faith is transmitted through initiation, direct revelation, song, story, dream, divination, and the living voice of elders — its authority resting in lineage, ceremony, and continuing revelation rather than a single written scripture.
  • Communion is sacramental. The deepest rites of the ministry are held with the utmost reverence — never casual, never recreational, always within tightly kept ceremony, with preparation before and tending after.
  • Reciprocity is binding. Offerings for the work are structured across tiered pathways so that no one is turned away from ceremony, healing, or counsel for reasons of financial incapacity. This is a religious practice, not a pricing strategy.
  • Consent and readiness are law. Informed consent, demonstrated readiness, careful screening, the refusal to facilitate where the work is not right for a person, and the duty to refer beyond our scope — these are binding precepts, not policies.
  • Confidentiality is holy ground. What is revealed within ceremony, divination, and pastoral counsel is kept, absolutely.
  • Reverence for all life. Truthfulness in counsel, tenderness toward the vulnerable, and the refusal of performance and spiritual bypass — only the kind of presence that lets a real thing happen.
II · Why This Ministry, Now

The nature of our times

We live in a season of severance — raised inside a story in which the Earth is inert, the dead are gone, and the sacred lives somewhere far away. The loneliness, the unspent grief, the unraveling of the living world: these are not separate problems. They are the symptoms of broken relationship.

And yet — everywhere, the gifts are waking. Sensitivity that will not be numbed. Dreams that arrive with instructions. A pull toward the unseen that no amount of productivity can silence. More people than at any time in living memory are being called — and they are waking inside a culture that has forgotten what they are for, without the villages, the elders, and the ceremonies that once received them.

‘The earth is asking for healers. The times are asking for those willing to go into the underworld and come back with something real. This work is my answer to that call — and it is an honor to walk that path alongside those who are ready.’ — Adi Marie

The Apothecary exists for this exact moment: to restore the old relationships, and to accompany those being called into their gifts. We hold, with our whole hearts, the premise of an entheogenically awakened society — so that what is received in the otherworlds returns as goodness in this one, shaping how we live and play and create and love.

The world is more porous than the world insists.

V · The Founder

Who I am by birth

The most important part of this is who and what I am, and have always been, by birth. There has been a dismemberment: an intentional deconstruction, a severance of ancestral roots, of lineages, of the ancient families of power through whom these kinds of gifts come forth into the world. It came through colonization, imperialism, and the diaspora, the diasporic dismemberment of these families of power, of these lineages of medicine people. There is no true teacher who holds the oral traditions of who and what we are intact. This is by blood, this is by nature, this is by the ancestry of my bones, this is by the ancestry of my spirit and of my soul.

That being said, otherworldly initiating bodies and forces have initiated me across the developmental thresholds of my life. They have deeply molded, shaped, and changed the infrastructure and very nature of my life, and the very nature of reality, in irrefutable ways. This has been extremely challenging to do on my own for the majority of my life. I did have support from different plant medicines, from different animal spirits, from different celestial and otherworldly guides, as well as my own soul, my own oversoul, and the different beings who have come forward at different times to help me further become.

It wasn't until recently that I was brought into a 40,000-year intact oral tradition, a closed lineage, by the Dagara people. Many people within that clan, within that family of medicine people, priests, priestesses, shamans, and diviners, have helped me ground and embody what has been moving through me and as me, like oceanic swells, onto the shorelines of this life.

Adi Marie

The Founder · The Path

How I came to this work

The calling arrived before the language for it. Long before training, before lineage, before the medicine basket — there was a felt sense of the world being more porous than the world insists. The dead were not gone. The land was not silent. The body knew things the mind had not been taught to translate.

The first formal door was the hands. Licensed bodywork in 2010, the somatic foundation under everything that followed. To work with the body honestly is to apprentice to a way of knowing that does not lie. From there the path opened — initiations into traditions that had been waiting, study with elders who recognized what I had been carrying since childhood, ceremony as the structure that could hold it.

Sixteen years on, the work has gathered itself into a practice. Solo sessions. Community ceremonies. Long arcs of accompaniment. The same intention threads through all of it: to meet what is real, to hold what others cannot hold, and to walk alongside those whose own calling is asking something of them they cannot yet name.

Where my people come from

I am part of a cultural refugee diaspora, descended from practices already syncretic through colonization and empire. In Central America, successive colonizations have diluted, and in places abjectly destroyed, my family’s relationship to animism and earth-based spirituality.

My father’s line · El Salvador

The Lenca people, as far as we can trace; the Maya, Pipil, and Ch’orti’; the Taíno and the Arawak.

My mother’s line · Cuba

The Caribbean, with its deep African influence: ancestors from Senegal and from the Canary Islands.

Iberia & the northern isles

Galician and Castilian roots and, further back, the Celtic peoples.

“No one of us in the modern world comes from a single cultural heritage. There’s a confluence of seas, of oceanic tides within me, and planes of remembering that I am synthesizing within my own life and bringing forward in my life’s expression.” — Adi Marie

Caribbean, African, and Mesoamerican ancestry is expressing itself through me, though I do not have the educational and cultural context that shaped me this way. These are things remembering themselves through me: my ancestors, sharing themselves through the remembrance of my bones.

Based in the canyon lands of Southern California, I offer private sessions and intensives in person, and I am available worldwide for virtual sessions, private travel engagements, and select community events.

XI · The Covenant

A ministry, not a marketplace

The Animist Apothecary is a ministry — a religious and ceremonial body, not a commercial enterprise. Its vocation is pastoral, and its duties are kept the way sacred duties are kept: with devotion, with discipline, and with accountability to lineage.

  • Sacramental ceremony. The facilitation of the deepest rites — with preparation, screening, and spiritual accompaniment before, and integration and aftercare long beyond.
  • Grief & ancestral work. The holding of grief, and the tending of the ancestral line through offerings, altar work, divination, and ceremony.
  • Rites of passage. Developmental arcs and threshold rites for those in spiritual transition — the crossings no one should walk alone.
  • Divination & counsel. Oracular divination and spiritual counsel, held in truthfulness and absolute confidence.
  • Laying on of hands. Ceremonial bodywork — sixteen years of licensed somatic and therapeutic skill, brought into ministerial service.
  • Transmission & tending. The teaching of those called into lineage, and the ongoing keeping of sacred fire, sacred water, sacred smoke, altars, and ceremonial grounds.

The ministry’s economic life is ordered by sacred reciprocity — tiered pathways of offering, held so that no one is turned away for reasons of financial incapacity. And it is held with a clear understanding, spoken plainly to every person who enters: this work is not medical or psychological treatment. It is a ceremonial and somatic journey into the depths of being — slow, relational, and sacred.

Those who give at the patron tier do more than enter the work — they endow it. Every arc held in abundance keeps a seat open at the fire for someone in a thin season. This is how a ministry sustains itself without becoming a marketplace: the circle carries its own.

Statement of Nature

The Animist Apothecary is a ministry of Gaian-based animism, a religious and spiritual body whose ceremonies, rites, divination, and counsel are acts of religious practice. They are not medical, psychological, or legal services. They do not diagnose or treat any condition, and they do not replace the care of a licensed professional.

Participation is by invitation, preparation, and demonstrated readiness, and is limited to adults. What is reciprocated is the seat, the facilitation, and the container. Offerings of reciprocity are not charitable donations and are not tax-deductible.

IV · The Conservatory of Esoteric Mysteries

How a practitioner is formed

This is the restoration of a way of life, a way of being in right relationship. Here you sit in tutelage to the other worlds: to their teachings, their initiations, and their attunements. The aim is to engage interdimensionally with skill, poise, and rapport, and with the artisan’s touch of inspired, skilled mastery.

We begin where every practice begins: with foundational skills and relationship. The School teaches in several studios at once. Each has its own ethos, body of knowledge, and skill set, and all of them follow the same path.

  1. I

    The Map

    The context every student needs first: the maps that have come before, known and unknown, and your own embodied reading of them.

  2. II

    Navigating the Map

    The key and the legend. What the symbols mean, how the terrains differ, and the principles and working equations that let you find your way.

  3. III

    Walking the Terrain

    First-person, lived exploration. Here you gain fluency: to communicate and be communicated with clearly, and to return to your everyday self.

  4. IV

    Field Training

    Practice as an intermediary, in service to community and to others.

  5. V

    Theory & Practicum

    Classroom study, applied practice, and the tests that show where you stand.

  6. VI

    Initiation

    The threshold, held in ceremony, after the clearing work that prepares you for it.

  7. VII

    Refinement & Mentorship

    Ongoing mentorship, at whatever depth and intimacy each person is here for.

III

Interdimensional Work

A field guide to the practices, principles, and protocols of journeying with the unseen realms, ancestral lineages, and spirit kin.

Interdimensional work is not a metaphor. It is the conscious crossing of thresholds — into ancestral memory, elemental kinship, dream territory, the worlds that ride alongside this one. It asks for protocol, posture, and patience.

What we are learning to do is move with reverence between rooms that most of modern life pretends are not there. The ones who go well are the ones who learn the manners of those rooms: how to enter, how to greet, how to listen, how to take leave.
