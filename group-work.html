Multi-Day · Initiatory · Cohorted

Ceremonial Retreats

Multi-day gatherings inside a larger group field — preparation, immersion, integration as one arc.

Often initiatory experiences, the retreats are for those ready to immerse themselves in otherworldly space for several days. A group spends a few weeks preparing virtually before gathering in person for deep inner and interdimensional work — coming together through multiple preparatory sessions, calls, and instructional classes so the in-person container coheres into a potent and beautiful experience. Several aftercare and integration calls are held for the group as well as one-on-one with each participant.

Phase 1 · Weeks Before

Preparation

Virtual preparatory sessions, calls, and instructional classes. The group coheres before it arrives.

Phase 2 · Several Days

Immersion

In person, on the land, in the otherworldly space. Deep inner and interdimensional work, held as one.

Phase 3 · Weeks After

Integration

Group aftercare calls and one-on-one integration sessions with each participant. The container closes slowly.
