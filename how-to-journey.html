How to Journey

The foundational skills within interdimensional work — learning to cross into the other worlds under your own power, and return whole.

Beginner – Intermediate

Tuesday, June 30 · 5:00–7:00pm PT · Online · 2 hours

Reserve Your Place

Before there were maps, there was the drum.

Long before any of our names, our people knew how to close their eyes, follow a steady beat past the edge of the ordinary world, and walk in the country of the spirits — to ask, to listen, and to carry something back.

This is the foundation beneath everything else. Ceremonial terrain, the elements, ancestral work, the deeper ceremony — all of it asks the same first thing of you: that you can leave ordinary consciousness on purpose, cross into the other worlds, and return whole. That single capacity is the journey. Learn it, and every other door opens.

It asks for almost nothing — your breath, your attention, and a willingness to go. Nothing is taken. The drum is the only medicine.

Why Begin Here

The first doorway

Journeying isn't one practice among many. It's the root the others grow from — the threshold every form of this work requires.

  • The master skill of crossing. Your own breath and a drum — not a substance, not a teacher's hand. The most portable, lifelong key there is.
  • It builds the inner senses. The seeing, hearing, and knowing that every interdimensional practice runs on are trained here, first.
  • It teaches the safety architecture. How to enter, stay oriented, and return clean — the grammar that keeps all deeper work sound.
  • It introduces your first allies. The helping spirits you meet in the journey become your guides for everything that follows.

The Evening

What we'll do together

Over two hours, I'll teach you the practice itself — and you'll journey live, drummed, because you don't learn to cross by hearing about it. You learn by doing it, held.

  • How to settle the body and cross the threshold safely
  • Riding the drumbeat into the lower world
  • Meeting a helping spirit — a power animal, a guide, an ally
  • Asking your question, and learning to receive what answers
  • Returning clean, and tending what you were given
