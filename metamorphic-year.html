The Counsel

One year. One gathered counsel.

A premium community cultivation program for those whose calling is to seed and lead communities in their own regions. Curated cohorts of visionary leaders who come together for one year of intentional cultivation — in their own traditions, rituals, and ceremonies.

This is not a curriculum to be consumed. This is a sustained gathering of leaders, councilors, and community-builders, woven into one year of shared practice. Each member tends their own region; each member tends the counsel itself.

For Whom

Those ready to build the village.

Founders, community organizers, faith leaders, healers, teachers, and visionaries with established practices — who feel the call to gather, host, lead, and steward the regenerative cultures their lands need.
