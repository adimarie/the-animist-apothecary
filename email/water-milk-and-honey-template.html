Begin the Intake

An invitation

Where might this work be needed?

This work travels by word of mouth, and by the right rooms finding it. So I am asking you, plainly —

If you know of a place where this work would be of benefit,

a place in your community to host a gathering,

or a need within your field that calls for it —

please reach out and let me know. The field is woven by hands like yours.
