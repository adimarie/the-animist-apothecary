The Library

A shelf for the path

The books and guides this practice reaches for most — the ones handed across the table, pressed into hands after ceremony, returned to year after year. Gathered from the worlds this work lives in: Dagara cosmology, the animate earth, ceremony and ritual, the tending of grief, and the entheogenic path walked with reverence.

Dagara Cosmology & African Wisdom

The lineage’s own voice

  • Of Water and the Spirit — Malidoma Patrice SoméInitiation, exile, and return — the autobiography of the lineage’s great emissary to the West.
  • The Healing Wisdom of Africa — Malidoma Patrice SoméRitual, community, and purpose as medicine — the clearest map of Dagara cosmology in print.
  • Ritual: Power, Healing and Community — Malidoma Patrice SoméSmall and potent — why ceremony is not optional for human beings.
  • The Spirit of Intimacy — Sobonfu SoméAncestral teachings on love, community, and the sacred between people.
  • Welcoming Spirit Home — Sobonfu SoméRitual, belonging, and the village that receives a soul.
Animism & the Living Earth

The world as relative

  • Braiding Sweetgrass — Robin Wall KimmererReciprocity with the living world, written like a prayer.
  • The Spell of the Sensuous — David AbramHow language and perception root us in — or exile us from — the animate earth.
  • Sand Talk — Tyson YunkaportaAn Indigenous systems view that will quietly rearrange your furniture.
  • Plant Intelligence and the Imaginal Realm — Stephen Harrod BuhnerThe doors of perception, opened botanically.
  • The Unlikely Peace at Cuchumaquic — Martín PrechtelSeeds, culture, and keeping the sacred alive through dark times.
Ceremony, Ritual & Rites of Passage

The architecture of crossings

  • The Way of the Shaman — Michael HarnerThe classic Western doorway into journeying practice.
  • Shamanism: Archaic Techniques of Ecstasy — Mircea EliadeThe scholarly bedrock beneath the word itself.
  • The Four-Fold Way — Angeles ArrienWarrior, healer, teacher, visionary — a cross-cultural grammar of practice.
  • The Rites of Passage — Arnold van GennepWhere the language of thresholds began.
  • Long Life, Honey in the Heart — Martín PrechtelVillage ritual life in full color — what ceremony is for.
Grief & Tending the Soul

  • The Smell of Rain on Dust — Martín PrechtelGrief and praise, braided as one.
  • When Things Fall Apart — Pema ChödrönGroundlessness as a path, not an emergency.
  • Healing Through the Dark Emotions — Miriam GreenspanAlchemy for despair, fear, and grief.
  • Ancestral Medicine — Daniel FoorRitual repair for the lines that hold us — lineage healing taught with rigor and heart.
The Entheogenic Path

Communion, studied and honored

  • How to Change Your Mind — Michael PollanThe mainstream doorway — careful, curious, well-reported.
  • Sacred Knowledge — William A. RichardsA lifetime of research on entheogens and religious experience.
  • The Way of the Psychonaut — Stanislav GrofThe great cartographer of non-ordinary states.
  • The Doors of Perception — Aldous HuxleyThe modern West’s first honest report from the other side.
