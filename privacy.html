The Fine Print

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The Animist Apothecary is the ministry and practice of Adi Marie Lopez, based in Southern California. This page explains what we collect when you use this website or work with us, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to us at hello@theanimistapothecary.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, plus a small amount of information about how the site is used.

  • The letters (Water, Milk, and Honey). Your first and last name, email address, zip code, how you found us, and anything you add.
  • Begin Here and inquiry forms. Your name, email address, phone number, zip code, what you are carrying, and how you found us.
  • Booking a session. Your name, email address, and the time you choose, through Google’s appointment scheduling.
  • Intake forms for ceremony and immersions. Your contact details, location, emergency contact, dietary and access needs, intentions, and the health information you choose to share, such as conditions, medications, and past experiences, so that we can consider safety and fit.
  • The participant portal. What you share there and the agreements you sign.
  • Reciprocity. Contributions are made through Venmo. We see the name and amount Venmo shows us, never your card or bank details.
  • Visits to the site. Our own privacy-minded measurement: which pages are visited, time spent on them, how far people scroll, which sections are read and which links or cards are clicked or lingered over, the website that sent the visit, device type, and time zone.

What we do not collect

Our site measurement uses no advertising or tracking cookies, does not record IP addresses, does not record your screen, and never reads what you type into a form until you choose to send it. We do not use advertising networks or sell your information.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, and to send the letters you asked for.
  • To schedule, prepare for, and hold sessions, ceremonies, and gatherings, including caring for your safety.
  • To keep records of agreements and contributions.
  • To understand which parts of the site are helpful, so we can make it clearer.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Health information

Health information shared in an intake form is held in confidence. It is seen only by those who need it to hold your session or ceremony safely, and it is never used for marketing. You may ask us to delete it once it is no longer needed, apart from any record we are required to keep.

Who helps us

A few trusted services help us run the site and the practice. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase, which stores form submissions and portal records in a secure database.
  • Google, for email, appointment scheduling, and the fonts on this site.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare, which host and deliver the site. Like most web hosts, they keep standard technical logs.
  • Venmo, for contributions.
  • YouTube, when you choose to play an embedded video. YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that video.

Small notes in your browser

The site keeps a few small notes in your browser so it works smoothly, such as a random key for your visit that is cleared when you close the tab, whether you closed an announcement, and, in the portal, keeping you signed in. These are not used to follow you across other websites.

Do Not Track

Because our measurement uses no cookies and nothing that follows you from site to site, every visitor is treated the same way, whether or not a browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Your choices

  • Leave the letters at any time with the link at the bottom of every letter, or by writing to us.
  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us what we have shared and with whom.

Write to hello@theanimistapothecary.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will never treat you differently for asking.

Keeping it safe, and for how long

We use reasonable safeguards to protect what you share, and we keep it only as long as the work needs it, or as long as the law requires.

Young people

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top of the page.
